about me

Hey! I’m Chaitanya Toraskar, a Software Developer and Data Analyst with a passion for technology, problem-solving, and building things that make a difference.

I’m currently pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (B.Sc. IT) at Vartak College, University of Mumbai, where I’m continuously strengthening my foundations in computer science, software engineering, databases, and data analytics.

My interests lie at the intersection of Software Development, Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, and Technology. I enjoy taking ideas from concept to implementation — whether that’s developing full-stack web applications, designing efficient database architectures, building interactive business intelligence dashboards, or experimenting with intelligent AI-powered systems.

My technical journey revolves around Python, Django, JavaScript, React, Next.js, SQL, MySQL, and Power BI. I also work with tools like Git, GitHub, Excel, and modern AI development platforms.

Beyond coding, I have a creative side. My experience with graphic design, video editing, and visual content creation has helped me develop an eye for design and user experience. I believe that great technology isn’t just about functionality; it’s also about how people interact with it.

I began gaining professional experience as a Teaching Faculty and IT Assistant at I-Tech Computer Institute, where I taught 50+ students, worked with Advanced Excel and database concepts, created digital learning materials, and developed automated spreadsheets. This experience strengthened my technical communication, problem-solving, and ability to explain complex concepts simply.

I’m particularly fascinated by the possibilities of Artificial Intelligence and automation. I enjoy exploring emerging technologies, building experimental projects, understanding how systems work behind the scenes, and pushing beyond what I already know.

I believe in learning by building. Every project is an opportunity to explore a new technology, solve a real-world problem, and become a better developer.

My goal is to grow into a well-rounded technology professional, explore opportunities beyond my current boundaries, and build meaningful products that combine software engineering, data, and intelligent systems.

Always curious. Always building. Always learning.
